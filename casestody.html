The Brief

Casestody is a phone case and protective accessories brand selling through an online store and physical retail locations. The challenge was to stand out by positioning design and lifestyle—not just protection—as the selling point.

Timeline: 1+ year

Role: Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media Manager

Scope: Product design, brand visuals, content creation, social strategy & community management

What I Worked On

Visual Design

Product Photography

Styled, shot, and edited product photography for case launches, displays, and social content.

Content Strategy

Social-First Content

Developed Reels, carousels, and static posts mixing UGC-style content with polished product shots.

Campaign Design

Promotional Campaigns

Designed promotional flyers and social assets for campaigns including a World Cup bundle promotion.

Brand Direction

Visual Identity

Shaped the brand's visual consistency across colour, typography, layouts and visual hierarchy.

Community

Social Media Management

Managed the Instagram presence, community, product launches and content planning.

Collaboration

Influencer & Event Content

Coordinated influencer unboxings, in-store events and user-generated content.

Web

Website Management

Managed web updates, e-commerce product pages and digital platforms.

Strategy: Position Casestody as an accessory and lifestyle choice, not simply a protection commodity.

The Outcome

Over a year of consistent content creation and community engagement, Casestody developed a recognizable Instagram presence with strong product imagery, engaged followers and a clear visual direction.

The mix of lifestyle content, product-focused Reels, UGC-style posts and promotional campaigns kept the feed fresh while showing how the cases fit into everyday life.
