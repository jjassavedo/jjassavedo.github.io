Casestody

Phone case & accessories brand — graphic design, content creation & social media strategy

Building a visual identity and social presence for a phone accessories brand that competes on design, not just durability. Product photography, promotional campaigns, community engagement, and a consistent brand story across Instagram.

The Brief

Casestody is a phone case and protective accessories brand selling through an online store and physical retail locations. The challenge: stand out in a crowded category by positioning design and lifestyle—not just protection—as the selling point.

Timeline: 1+ year

Role: Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media Manager

Scope: Product design, brand visuals, content creation, social strategy & community management

What I Worked On

Visual Design

Product Photography

Styled, shot, and edited product photography for case launches, displays, and social content — creating a consistent visual language across the product range.

Content Strategy

Social-First Content

Developed Reels, carousels, and static posts that moved product and built community — mixing UGC-style content with polished product shots.

Campaign Design

Promotional Campaigns

Designed promotional flyers and social assets for campaigns including a World Cup bundle promotion — mixing bold red branding with product imagery.

Brand Direction

Visual Identity

Shaped the brand's visual consistency — color palette, typography, grid layouts, and visual hierarchy across all platforms.

Community

Social Media Management

Managed day-to-day Instagram presence, responded to community, coordinated product launches, and planned content calendars.

Collaboration

Influencer & Event Content

Coordinated influencer unboxings, in-store events, and user-generated content to drive social proof and brand reach.

Web

Website Management

Managed Casestody's web presence, including site updates, e-commerce product pages, and digital platforms.

Strategy: Position Casestody not as a protection commodity, but as an accessory and lifestyle choice. The visual identity needed to reflect design-forward thinking — variety in case designs, premium materials, personalization options — and build a community where customers felt like they were part of something intentional, not just buying a functional product.

Selected Work

Product design, promotional campaigns, and social content that positioned Casestody as a lifestyle brand, not just a functional accessory.

The Outcome

Over a year of consistent content creation and community engagement, Casestody grew into a recognizable brand on Instagram with strong product imagery, engaged followers, and a clear visual direction.

The mix of lifestyle content, product-focused Reels, UGC-style posts, and promotional campaigns created a social presence that felt authentic and kept the feed fresh. Followers weren't just seeing product shots—they were seeing how the cases fit into everyday life.
