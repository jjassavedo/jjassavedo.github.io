The Brief

Casestody is a phone accessories brand selling through its online store and physical retail locations. I was involved in the day-to-day creative work behind the brand, from product content and social media to campaigns and visual communication.

Timeline: 1+ year

Role: Content and Branding Officer

Scope: Content, branding, social media, product photography and campaigns

What I Worked On

Product Photography

Created and edited product photography for new products, launches, displays and social media.

Social Content

Created Reels, carousels and static posts that combined product-focused content with more natural, social-first ideas.

Promotional Campaigns

Designed promotional materials and social assets for campaigns, including seasonal offers and the World Cup bundle promotion.

Brand Visuals

Worked on the visual side of the brand across layouts, typography, colour and other branded content.

Social Media

Handled day-to-day content planning and social media work, from preparing posts to keeping the brand active and consistent.

Influencer & Event Content

Worked on influencer unboxings, in-store events and user-generated content to give the brand more ways to show its products.

Website

Worked on website updates, product pages and other digital content across the e-commerce side of the brand.

More Than Just a Phone Case: A lot of the work was about showing the products beyond their basic function. The content needed to make the cases feel like something people would actually want to carry, use and have around them.

That meant mixing product photography with lifestyle content, Reels, campaigns, collaborations and everyday social content.

The Outcome

Over the time I worked with Casestody, the brand built a stronger and more consistent presence across its social platforms. I contributed to the content, visuals and day-to-day creative work that kept the brand active and gave its products more ways to be seen.

The work ranged from polished product visuals to Reels, campaigns, influencer content and everyday social posts, giving Casestody a wider visual language to work with.
