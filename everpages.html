Everpages creates personalised products that turn photographs and memories into keepsakes, from colouring books and animated books to photo art and prints. My current focus is managing Instagram advertising to help the brand reach more potential customers and generate enquiries.

My contribution.

I created three launch posts for Everpages and provided photography and photo-editing support for a short period. I now focus on managing the brand’s Instagram ads. Over a two-week advertising period, the campaign generated 18,137 views, reached 12,331 accounts and recorded 3,227 engagements, alongside up to 30 message leads as reported by the client-side tracking.
