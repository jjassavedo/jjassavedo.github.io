The Brief

Harmatan is my own creative and marketing agency. It brings design, content, direction and marketing together to help brands develop a stronger visual identity and marketing presence.

Role: Founder

Position: Creative & Marketing Agency

Scope: Visual identity, content, creative direction and marketing

What Harmatan Is Built Around

01 · Design

Visual Identity

Helping brands develop a visual identity that gives their work a clear and recognizable presence.

02 · Content

Content

Bringing content creation into the same system as the brand and its wider marketing presence.

03 · Direction

Creative Direction

Connecting ideas, design and execution so the creative work moves in one intentional direction.

04 · Marketing

Marketing

Bringing marketing thinking together with design and content rather than treating them as separate outputs.

Positioning: Harmatan brings design, content, direction and marketing together under one roof — built to help brands develop both their visual identity and their marketing presence.
