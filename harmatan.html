The Position

For brands who want to matter to the new African generation, HARMATAN turns cultural fluency into commercial results — because the team didn't study the culture, it comes from it.

The Story

From Editorial
To Studio.

HARMATAN started as a digital magazine because the founder wanted to create a space around fashion, finance and culture. That became a starting point, not a destination.

A few months later, the decision was made to build HARMATAN Studio — a creative practice that could work directly with brands and founders, turning cultural understanding into identity, growth and content.

HARMATAN Editorial remains part of the DNA. It is now the internal content voice and engine of the studio, showing up through the ideas, takes and conversations HARMATAN publishes.

What changed

The publication became the point of view.

The studio is the business. Editorial is the voice. The goal is to build brands that matter to the people they are actually trying to reach.
