The Brief

TropiBite is a food and beverage brand specializing in tropical coconut water, pineapple and mango products.

The project focused on building a recognizable brand presence from the ground up — creating consistent visual identity, product photography, and social content strategy.

Role: Graphic Designer & Social Media Manager

Scope: Product photography, brand design, content creation, social media strategy and community management

Content: Product showcases, launch campaigns, lifestyle content, UGC and promotional material

What I Worked On

Brand Design

Visual Identity

Developed the brand color palette (mint green, bright yellow, peach, cream) and visual system for consistent product presentation.

Photography

Product Photography

Created product photography showcasing coconut water, pineapple, mango and lifestyle imagery.

Campaigns

Launch Campaigns

Designed promotional campaigns and countdowns for product launches with engaging CTAs.

Content

Social Content Strategy

Built content calendar with Reels, product features, UGC and lifestyle content.

Strategy: Build a recognizable brand presence across social by connecting visual design, product storytelling and community engagement.

The Outcome

TropiBite established a fresh, cohesive brand presence across social platforms with consistent visual identity, engaged community and growing product visibility.

The work brought product presentation, campaign design and social content together into a recognizable visual system.
