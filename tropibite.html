The Brief

TropiBite is a beverage brand built around fresh, healthy tropical drinks. The work focused on giving the brand a consistent visual presence across product content, social media and promotional campaigns.

Role: Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media

Focus: Product content, social creative, campaigns and community

What I Worked On

Product

Product Photography

Created clean, bright product visuals using tropical backgrounds and fruit-led compositions.

Video

Video Reels

Created short-form video content around products, people and community moments.

UGC

User-Generated Content

Built content around real people enjoying the drinks in everyday settings.

Lifestyle

Lifestyle Content

Created social content around events, parties and moments where the drinks naturally fit in.

Campaigns

Launch Campaigns

Developed campaign content including “YES WE ARE BACK” and countdown posts.

Social

Social Media Management

Managed Instagram strategy and posting while keeping the brand's visual direction consistent.

Community

Community Engagement

Used feedback-led content to encourage interaction and build familiarity with the brand.

Promotion

Promotional Campaigns

Created promotional communication with direct calls to action such as “ORDER NOW”.

Creative direction: Make TropiBite feel fresh, social and easy to associate with real moments — not just a drink on a shelf.

Results

The strongest content combined product visibility with social and community-led moments. A peak video reached 15.4K views.

Product content: Up to 3,623 views

Lifestyle content: Up to 2,095 views
