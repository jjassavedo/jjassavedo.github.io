About

01

I'm a Graphic Designer and Creative with a hands-on approach to building brands and creating content. Graphic design is the foundation of my work, but my role goes beyond creating individual visuals.

I develop concepts, shape creative direction, plan and execute shoots, create photo and video content, edit and colour-grade, manage social platforms, and contribute to the wider marketing strategy — taking an idea from first concept to final piece of content.

05 · Creative Approach

Design, content and social are not separate things.

A strong brand should look good, communicate clearly and give people a reason to pay attention. My process can start with a logo, campaign idea, social post or a simple brief — and develop into a complete creative direction, from concept and design to production, editing and publishing.

That's what allows me to work not just as a designer, but as a hands-on creative.
