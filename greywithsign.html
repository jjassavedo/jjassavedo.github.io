The Brief

Help build and manage a social presence where the content is simple, funny and rooted in everyday observations.

Role: Social Media Manager & Creative Support

Content format: Sign-holding street content, short-form social posts and topical humour

Audience: Ghanaian and wider social-media audiences

What I Worked On

Ideas

Content Ideation

Developed simple, relatable sign concepts around humour, opinions and everyday life.

Production

Content Direction

Helped shape the format and execution of the sign-based content.

Social

Social Media Management

Managed the social side of the content and kept the page consistent.

Publishing

Publishing

Turned finished ideas into posts built for engagement and shareability.

The idea: Put a simple sign in a real place, give people something worth reacting to, and let the moment do the work.

Content That Worked

The page uses a repeatable format: people, places and handwritten signs with quick observations or opinions. The screenshots show posts ranging from 355K and 92K views to smaller posts in the thousands.
