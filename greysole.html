Greysole

Minimal apparel & lifestyle brand — graphic design, content creation & social media

Building a lifestyle brand around the philosophy that fashion should be comfortable, accessible, and intentional. Through clean product photography, curated lifestyle imagery, and strategic social presence, Greysole positioned itself as the go-to for "soft essentials for everyday" wear.

The Brief

Greysole is a minimal apparel brand selling shirts, socks, caps, and sliders — positioned at the intersection of fashion and comfort. The challenge: build a visual identity and community that competes on design sensibility, not pricing, while expanding from socks into a full lifestyle brand.

Timeline: 1+ year

Role: Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media Manager

Scope: Brand direction, product photography, content strategy & social management

What I Worked On

Product Photography

Clean Apparel Shots

Shot and styled product photography for apparel — shirts, socks, caps — using diverse models and neutral backgrounds to position the brand as inclusive and modern.

Lifestyle Content

Social-First Creative

Created lifestyle content mixing product showcases with behind-the-scenes and community moments — building a feed that told a brand story, not just moved inventory.

Visual Identity

Brand Direction

Established a minimal, high-contrast visual identity — clean typography, monochromatic product shots, and a yellow accent color that made the brand unmistakable.

Campaign Strategy

Promotional Content

Designed and executed promotional campaigns, including a World Cup bundle campaign — balancing promotional messaging with the brand's refined aesthetic.

Content Planning

Social Media Management

Planned and executed a consistent social strategy across Instagram, from daily stories to Reels to carousel posts — maintaining visual consistency while keeping the feed fresh.

Community

Brand Building

Managed community engagement, responded to customers, coordinated product launches, and built a culture around Greysole's philosophy of casual, intentional living.

Web

Website Management

Managed Greysole's web presence, including site updates, product pages, and digital presence across platforms.

Philosophy: Greysole isn't just about selling apparel—it's about positioning a lifestyle. The visual identity needed to reflect minimalism and intentionality. Every photo, every post, every caption reinforced the idea that Greysole products were for people who value quality, comfort, and a no-nonsense approach to getting dressed.

Selected Work

Product photography, lifestyle content, and social strategy across Instagram feed and Reels.

The Outcome

Greysole evolved from a socks-focused brand into a full lifestyle apparel brand with a clear visual voice and engaged community. The consistent visual direction—clean product photography, minimal design, and thoughtful brand storytelling—built trust and positioned Greysole as a deliberate choice, not a commodity.

The brand's social presence grew to 12K+ followers with sustained engagement, proving that in a crowded apparel market, a refined visual identity and authentic brand voice cut through the noise. Greysole's followers were engaged because they believed in the philosophy, not just the products.
