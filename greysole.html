The Brief

Greysole is a minimal apparel and lifestyle brand selling shirts, socks, caps and sliders. The challenge was to build a visual identity and community that competed on design sensibility, not pricing.

Timeline: 1+ year

Role: Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media Manager

Scope: Brand direction, product photography, content strategy & social management

What I Worked On

Product Photography

Clean Apparel Shots

Shot and styled product photography for apparel using diverse models and neutral backgrounds.

Lifestyle Content

Social-First Creative

Created lifestyle content mixing product showcases with behind-the-scenes and community moments.

Visual Identity

Brand Direction

Established a minimal, high-contrast visual identity with clean typography and a distinctive yellow accent.

Campaign Strategy

Promotional Content

Designed and executed promotional campaigns while keeping the brand's refined aesthetic.

Content Planning

Social Media Management

Planned and executed Instagram content across stories, Reels and carousel posts.

Community

Brand Building

Managed community engagement, product launches and a culture around casual, intentional living.

Web

Website Management

Managed site updates, product pages and digital presence across platforms.

Philosophy: Position Greysole as a lifestyle, not just apparel — with every visual reinforcing comfort, quality and intentionality.

The Outcome

A consistent visual system and content approach helped Greysole build a recognizable lifestyle presence across social platforms.

The mix of product photography, lifestyle content, campaigns and community management kept the brand coherent while allowing the feed to stay fresh.
