The Brief

Greysole is a minimal apparel and lifestyle brand selling shirts, socks, caps and sliders. My work covered the creative side of the brand, from product photography and social content to campaigns, branding and day-to-day digital work.

Timeline: 1+ year

Role: Content and Branding Officer

Scope: Branding, content, social media, product photography and campaigns

What I Worked On

Clean Apparel Shots

Created and edited product photography for apparel, using different models, settings and compositions to present the products clearly.

Social-First Creative

Created lifestyle content, Reels, carousels and other social content that mixed product-focused work with more natural moments around the brand.

Brand Direction

Worked on the visual direction of the brand across typography, layouts, colour and other branded content.

Promotional Content

Designed promotional materials and campaign content while keeping the visual style consistent across different promotions.

Social Media Management

Planned and created content for Instagram, from Stories and Reels to carousel posts and everyday social content.

Brand Building

Worked on community-focused content, product launches and other creative work that helped give the brand a more recognizable presence.

Website Management

Worked on website updates, product pages and other digital content across the brand's online presence.

Making the Brand Feel Like More Than Apparel: The goal was to make Greysole feel like more than a clothing brand. The visuals needed to communicate the lifestyle around the products while keeping the brand clean, simple and recognizable.

That meant balancing polished product work with lifestyle content, campaigns and more everyday social moments.

The Outcome

The work helped Greysole maintain a consistent visual presence across its social platforms while giving the brand different ways to show its products and personality.

From product photography and social content to campaigns and website work, I was involved across different parts of the brand's day-to-day creative output.
