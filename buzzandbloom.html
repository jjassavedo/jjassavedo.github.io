Buzz & Bloom

Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media Manager · Beverage Startup

A creative and social media partnership built from concept through execution — shaping the visual presence, content production and Instagram strategy for a growing beverage brand.

The Brief

Buzz & Bloom is a growing beverage startup. The work centered on building a consistent visual and social presence while turning ideas into finished content for the brand.

Role: Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media Manager

Scope: Shoot planning, photography, creative concepts, content production and Instagram management

Focus: Building a consistent visual and social presence for a growing beverage brand

What I Worked On

01 · Planning

Shoot Planning

Planned creative shoots around the brand's content needs, moving ideas from concept into production.

02 · Content

Photography

Produced visual content designed to give the beverage brand a recognizable and consistent presence.

03 · Direction

Creative Concepts

Developed creative concepts that connected the product with a clear visual story.

04 · Social

Instagram Management

Managed the brand's Instagram presence and translated the creative direction into a consistent social output.

Approach: Keep the creative process practical and connected — plan the shoot, produce the assets, then carry the same thinking through the social platform.
