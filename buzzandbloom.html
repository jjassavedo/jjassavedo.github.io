The Brief

Buzz & Bloom is a growing cocktails and mocktails brand focused on its products and social-first content.

The work centered on building a consistent visual presence and turning the brand's products and ideas into content people could actually engage with.

Role: Graphic Designer, Content Creator & Social Media Manager

Scope: Shoot planning, photography, creative concepts, content production and Instagram management

Content: Product showcases, lifestyle content, promotions and campaign-led social posts
